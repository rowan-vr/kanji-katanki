Kanji Katanki — learn kanji from your vocabulary

Create kanji recognition and writing cards from the Japanese vocabulary you already study in Anki. Kanji Katanki works offline and keeps recognition and writing progress separate.

Easy setup. Start with the words you already study.

The setup wizard walks you through vocabulary sources, destination decks, and card appearance. Choose your decks and expression fields, then preview and approve your first kanji cards.

Your cards, your way.

Study recognition, writing, or both. Choose which examples appear, adjust their limits, and toggle furigana independently on the front and back. Writing cards use meanings and masked examples from your vocabulary.

Flip the card for readings, meanings, stroke playback, components, and vocabulary examples with their saved learning states.

Bring your old cards. Keep your progress.

Already using Kanji GOD or another kanji deck? Map supported source decks, note types, and character fields, then review the conversion. Card IDs, scheduling, and review history are preserved, with an automatic backup and Anki Undo.

Settings that fit your collection.

Pick your vocabulary sources, include subdecks, choose where new cards go, and control automatic refresh. Dictionaries work offline out of the box, with optional updates and local overrides when you want them.

Watch your kanji turn green.

Explore a colour-coded kanji grid, compare progress across school grades and unofficial JLPT lists, and inspect individual characters. Recognition and writing are tracked separately, so knowing a kanji does not automatically count as being able to write it. Review-history charts are one click away.

Getting started

  1. Install the add-on and restart desktop Anki.
  2. Follow the first-run setup to choose your vocabulary sources and destination decks.
  3. Use Katanki → Refresh and generate Kanji Cards, review the candidates, and approve creation.

Restart setup from Katanki → Settings… → Refresh → Restart setup…. Advanced settings, dictionary controls, and statistics are available from the Katanki menu.

Existing cards and backups

Back up your collection before converting existing cards. Use Katanki → Migrate Existing Cards… to review supported conversions. Migration also creates an automatic collection backup and supports Anki Undo. Note IDs may change; unmapped sibling cards remain in their original notes. Cloze sources and unencoded Parts mappings are unsupported.

Refresh can overwrite managed example fields according to your saved settings. Generated cards remain studyable without the add-on or source decks. The add-on itself runs in desktop Anki; it does not run on mobile.

All offline. From setup to study.

Dictionaries, vocabulary suggestions, and stroke diagrams come bundled. Set up your sources, generate cards, explore statistics, and review without an internet connection. No extra account or separate downloads needed to get started. Generated cards stay studyable without the add-on or source decks, too. Internet access is only needed if you choose to download dictionary updates.
